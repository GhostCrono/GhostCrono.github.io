Floraforma Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Floraforma is a puzzle game made by Ryan Camp. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: Floraforma does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: levels completed, high scores, Petals, the flowers, animals and decorations in your Journal, and your settings such as volume. This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account so it carries over to your other iPhones and iPads. Apple stores this copy under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it. You can turn this off in the iPhone Settings app under your name > iCloud.

What we don't do

Floraforma has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone, it never reads or views the photos in your photo library, and it does not connect to any server run by us.

Photo Mode and your photo library

Photo Mode lets you take a picture of your cottage. When you take one, Floraforma asks iOS for permission to add photos to your photo library, and only saves the picture you took there. This add-only permission does not let the game see, read or change any other photos in your library, and the pictures you take are never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this permission off at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Photos.

Game Center

If a future version of Floraforma adds Apple Game Center achievements and you are signed in to Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center profile and achievements under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center information beyond what Game Center shows in the game.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
